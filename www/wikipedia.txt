Tools & usage (rough,wip):
This handcranked database is tools agnostic,  but is designed for Sage and Punk!
Sage gives you different text modes to view it in, and Punk gives 2D modes to vi
ew it in. You can also plug both into any SAK file on the internet,   and expand
the capabilities from simple lookups to more advanced behaviors.  I use it as an 
immersive learning project to see my own weaknesses in how I  use any techniques
available to me.
  The conjunction of Sage, SAK and Punk also comes down to compositing and morph
ing data as it evolves,  but not losing sight of the legacy of expandability and 
explorative prospects. The first thing you might want to do with Sage is to conv
ert this document to a text version, instead of seeing so many URL addresses, bu
t scope it how you want to.
So, it's still me creating my own self contained internet, over a LAN, to unplug 
from the freight train forever.
